CYD CHESS

Flash chess onto your ESP32-2432S028

This browser can't talk to serial ports. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop. Serial access needs https or localhost. Start this page with flash.bat / flash.py.
  1. Plug the CYD in with a USB data cable.
  2. Press Connect & Flash and pick the CYD's serial port (CH340 / USB Serial).
  3. Tick Erase device to wipe the flash first, then press Install.

If it can't connect, hold the BOOT button on the CYD while pressing Install.
